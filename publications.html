Publications

First author on all four journal articles.

  1. [1]

    Extended Road-Aware Line-of-Sight Probability Model for Urban Air Mobility

    A. Abu Zaid, B. E. Y. Belmekki, M.-S. Alouini. IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems, vol. 61, no. 5, pp. 15014–15020, 2025.

    Shows where a widely used line-of-sight model misjudges interference from ground vehicles, then replaces it with one that accounts for city layout and where vehicles sit on the street.

    Best Paper Award, ICAO, 2024. DOI

  2. [2]

    Aerial-Terrestrial Heterogeneous Networks for Urban Air Mobility: A Performance Analysis

    A. Abu Zaid, B. E. Y. Belmekki, M.-S. Alouini. IEEE Open Journal of Vehicular Technology, vol. 6, pp. 912–926, 2025.

    Calculates how reliably a combined aerial and terrestrial network can serve air taxis flying along fixed corridors, in both directions of the link, and checks the math against simulation.

    Poster at IEEE CTW 2024, Banff, Canada. DOI

  3. [3]

    eVTOL Communications and Networking in UAM: Requirements, Key Enablers, and Challenges

    A. Abu Zaid, B. E. Y. Belmekki, M.-S. Alouini. IEEE Communications Magazine, vol. 61, no. 8, pp. 154–160, 2023.

    Sets out what electric air taxis need from a network, the technologies that could deliver it, and a case study comparing terrestrial and aerial infrastructure.

    Poster at IEEE EuCNC & 6G Summit 2023, Gothenburg, Sweden. DOI

  4. [4]

    Aerial-Aided mmWave VANETs Using NOMA: Performance Analysis, Comparison, and Insights

    A. Abu Zaid, B. E. Y. Belmekki, M.-S. Alouini. IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, vol. 73, no. 4, pp. 4742–4758. First published 2023.

    Compares tethered flying platforms with roadside units for connecting vehicles, and ends in two tables that tell a designer which platform and transmission scheme suit a given situation.

    Poster at IEEE EuCNC & 6G Summit 2022, Grenoble, France. DOI

  5. PhD

    Urban Air Mobility Communications: Performance Analysis and Design

    Doctoral dissertation, King Abdullah University of Science and Technology, December 2025. Advisor: Prof. Mohamed-Slim Alouini.
