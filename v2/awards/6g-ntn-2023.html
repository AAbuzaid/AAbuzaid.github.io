NTN for Urban Air Mobility Coverage: A Novel Probability of Line-of-Sight Model
Authors

Contest page Webinar recording

The paper

The paper proposes a new line-of-sight probability model and uses it to analyze how non-terrestrial networks can provide coverage for urban air mobility.

The contest

The contest asked for an innovative idea on 6G satellite systems and non-terrestrial networks, written as a short paper of up to five pages. It was open to students and to researchers who had received their PhD in 2021, 2022, or 2023, aged 20 to 35, with one entry per person. Entries were accepted from 13 October to 10 November 2023 and reviewed by a technical program committee of researchers from Europe, North America, the Middle East, and Asia.

Four prizes were given: first place, second place, and two third places. The winners presented their papers at an IEEE Future Networks webinar on 20 December 2023.
