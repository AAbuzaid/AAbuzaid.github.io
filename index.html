Abu Zaid

Wireless networks for aircraft,
from street level to orbit.

I am a wireless communications researcher working on non-terrestrial networks and urban air mobility. I received my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KAUST in December 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Mohamed-Slim Alouini. My doctoral research modeled the coverage and reliability of wireless networks for electric air taxis, using stochastic geometry and simulation. From October 2025 to March 2026, I was an Autonomous Systems Intern at Boeing in Riyadh.

Research interests: AI-enabled non-terrestrial networks; HAPS and UAV communications; foundation models and LLM agents for network automation; stochastic geometry and performance analysis of aerial-terrestrial networks; learning-based network optimization; aerial perception for autonomous missions.

Research

Research page

2026

Desert search and rescue with high-altitude platforms and drones

3rd place out of 81, NTN 2026 competition.

Cameras on a high-altitude platform (HAPS) scan a wide area, onboard AI sends only vehicle detections over a modest satellite link, and drones confirm each candidate.

2025

Aerial and terrestrial networks for air taxis

IEEE Open Journal of Vehicular Technology, 2025. 3rd place, Four Minute Thesis, IEEE ICC 2025.

Calculates how reliably a combined aerial and terrestrial network can serve air taxis flying along fixed corridors, in both directions of the link.

2024 to 2025

A road-aware line-of-sight model for urban air mobility

ICAO Best Paper Award, 2024. IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems, 2025.

Shows where a widely used line-of-sight model misjudges interference from ground vehicles, then replaces it with one that accounts for city layout and where vehicles sit on the street.

2023

A line-of-sight model for non-terrestrial coverage of urban air mobility

3rd place, IEEE Future Networks 6G and NTN contest, 2023.

A new line-of-sight probability model, used to analyze how non-terrestrial networks can provide coverage for urban air mobility.

2023

Communications and networking for eVTOL aircraft

IEEE Communications Magazine, 2023.

Sets out what electric air taxis need from a network, the technologies that could deliver it, and a case study comparing terrestrial and aerial infrastructure.

2023

Aerial-aided mmWave vehicular networks using NOMA

IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology, 2023.

Compares tethered flying platforms with roadside units for connecting vehicles, and ends in two tables that tell a designer which platform and transmission scheme suit a given situation.
